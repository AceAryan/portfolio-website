Microsoft PowerToys — Awake tray time shortcuts

PR #51870 Issue #49529

The Awake utility's tray menu only offered keep-awake intervals of up to 2 hours, and custom durations could only be set by hand-editing settings.json. This PR adds 4, 8 and 12 hour defaults and a new Settings section where users can add, remove and reset their own tray time shortcuts.
